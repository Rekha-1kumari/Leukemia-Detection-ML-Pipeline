CELLULAR MICRO-ENVIRONMENT SIMULATION

Chimeric Antigen Receptor (CAR-T) Cell CD19 Docking & Lysis

Malignant ALL Blast (CD19+) Reprogrammed CAR-T Cell Lysed / Apoptotic Cell

Cellular Mechanism of Action

1
T-Cell Leukapheresis

Native cytotoxic T-lymphocytes are harvested from the patient's peripheral blood via apheresis.

2
Lentiviral CAR Engineering

A disarmed viral vector introduces a synthetic Chimeric Antigen Receptor engineered to bind surface CD19 antigens unique to B-precursor blasts.

3
Perforin/Granzyme Cytolysis

Upon receptor docking, the CAR-T cell forms an immunological synapse and discharges perforin and granzyme, inducing apoptotic cell death.

FDA Indications Relapsed/Refractory B-ALL
Complete Remission 70% – 85% Cohort Response
ONCOLOGY STANDARD OF CARE

Multi-Agent Phased Chemotherapy Protocols

First-Line Remission Therapy
PHASE 1

Remission Induction

Duration: 4 to 6 Weeks

Primary Objective: Rapidly clear the blood and marrow of identifiable leukemic blasts to reach Complete Remission (< 5% marrow blasts).

  • Vincristine (mitotic spindle arrest)
  • Dexamethasone or Prednisone (lymphoblast cytolysis)
  • PEG-L-asparaginase (asparagine amino acid depletion)
  • Daunorubicin / Doxorubicin (anthracycline DNA intercalation)
PHASE 2

Consolidation / Intensification

Duration: 4 to 6 Months

Primary Objective: Eliminate residual sub-microscopic clones (Measurable Residual Disease, MRD) to prevent early relapse.

  • High-Dose Methotrexate with Leucovorin rescue
  • Cytarabine (Ara-C DNA synthesis inhibitor)
  • 6-Mercaptopurine (6-MP antimetabolite)
PHASE 3

CNS Prophylaxis

Concurrent Across Treatment

Primary Objective: Protect the central nervous system sanctuary behind the blood-brain barrier from leukemic seeding.

  • Intrathecal Methotrexate via lumbar puncture
  • Intrathecal Hydrocortisone and Cytarabine
PHASE 4

Maintenance Therapy

Duration: 2 to 3 Years

Primary Objective: Continuous low-dose oral suppression preventing late recurrence while enabling resumption of school or work.

  • Daily oral 6-Mercaptopurine
  • Weekly oral Methotrexate
  • Periodic pulses of Vincristine & Corticosteroids
CURATIVE CELL THERAPY

Allogeneic Hematopoietic Stem Cell Transplantation (HSCT)

High-Risk & Relapsed Cases
🧪

1. Conditioning

High-dose myeloablative chemotherapy and total body irradiation destroys the diseased leukemic bone marrow and suppresses host immunity.

🩸

2. Stem Cell Infusion

HLA-matched healthy donor CD34+ hematopoietic stem cells are infused intravenously through a central venous catheter.

🦴

3. Marrow Engraftment

Donor stem cells home to bone marrow cavities, begin proliferating, and reconstitute healthy red cells, white cells, and platelets within 2–3 weeks.

🛡️

4. Graft-vs-Leukemia

Donor T-lymphocytes actively identify and eradicate residual host malignant clones, providing lifelong immunological surveillance.

PRECISION MEDICINE

Targeted Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors (TKI)

Philadelphia Chromosome Positive (Ph+) ALL

The BCR-ABL1 Fusion Oncogene

A reciprocal chromosomal translocation $t(9;22)(q34;q11)$ produces the Philadelphia chromosome, generating a constitutively active tyrosine kinase that blocks apoptosis and drives malignant proliferation.

First Generation: Imatinib (Gleevec)
Second Generation: Dasatinib, Nilotinib
Third Generation: Ponatinib (overcomes T315I gatekeeper mutation)

Mechanism of Action

  • • Competitive ATP Binding: The inhibitor binds specifically into the ATP-binding cleft of the BCR-ABL1 kinase domain.
  • • Signal Shutdown: Inhibits tyrosine phosphorylation of downstream survival pathways (JAK/STAT, PI3K/AKT, and RAS/MAPK).
  • • Apoptosis Re-activation: Allows malignant lymphoblasts to undergo normal programmed cell death while sparing healthy tissues.
